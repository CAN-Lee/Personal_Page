I am a Ph.D. student in Robotics at <a href="https://en.nankai.edu.cn/">Nankai University</a>, Tianjin, China. I will soon join <a href="https://www.shlab.org.cn/">Shanghai AI Lab</a> as a research intern. Previously, I was a research intern at <a href="https://www.a4x.io/">A4x (Rightly Robotics)</a>. I received my master's degree from <a href="https://english.cqu.edu.cn/">Chongqing University</a>.

My current research interests focus on **world models**, including visual reconstruction, differentiable physics simulation, and generation from videos/images. My earlier research interests were in **robotics**, particularly visual-tactile-force manipulation of deformable objects.
